Ordinary gamepads now work, sharper textures can be downloaded, and several
faults are fixed that only showed up after playing for a while.

WHAT TO TRY

• Gamepad: pair a standard controller pad and play without spatial controllers.
  Menus with the right stick, select on R2, aim by looking. Weapons cycle with
  the d-pad while R1 is held, powers the same way with L1.
• Spatial controllers: tracking was reworked for older system versions. If your
  controllers used to report buttons but never moved, this is the build for
  that. The launcher window now shows what the system reports at the bottom —
  a screenshot of that line helps us a lot if something is missing.
• Texture resolution: pick Default, 2x or 4x in the launcher window. The app
  downloads the packages itself; 4x is several gigabytes and is explicitly an
  experiment. Watch the load times, and whether the game stops on larger maps —
  that is exactly what we want to hear about.
• Carrying on after dying: die, reload, then use lifts and switches. That used
  to stop working, and should hold now.
• While you are at it: do not expect to keep firing right after a load. The
  trigger is deliberately released once, so press it again.

WHAT WE KNOW

Long level loads take a while — the window stays black meanwhile. With the 4x
package they take considerably longer.
