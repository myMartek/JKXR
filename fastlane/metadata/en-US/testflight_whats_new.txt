This version lets you play with an ordinary gamepad, download sharper textures,
and fixes several annoying bugs.

NEW

• Play with a gamepad: You no longer need spatial controllers. Pair a standard
  controller pad and you are ready to go. You aim by looking and fire with R2.
  Switch weapons with the d-pad while holding R1 — and powers the same way with
  L1.

• Sharper textures: The launcher window now lets you choose between normal
  resolution, 2x and 4x. The app downloads the matching package for you. 4x is
  very large and still an experiment — please tell us whether your game stays
  stable with it.

• The launcher shows your controllers: At the bottom of the window you can now
  see which controllers the system recognises. If yours are not working, a
  screenshot of that line helps us a great deal.

FIXED

• Weapon scopes work properly again. The scope image appeared in a different
  place in each eye and would not merge, thin lines such as the crosshair
  showed up doubled, and shots landed far above the target. The scope now
  fills your whole view, sits still, and you aim with both hands, the way you
  would shoulder a rifle.

• In the game you now only see the character's hands. The system used to
  show your real hands and forearms on top of them, which was confusing
  especially in close combat.

• Loading the autosave at the start of Artus Detention now plays the complete
  vehicle camera sequence. Afterwards, Kyle can move as intended.

• Cut-scenes can be skipped again. If you skipped the camera sequence at the
  end of the training with the trigger, all you got was a black screen, and
  moments later the game quit. The completion menu now appears as intended.

• In that completion menu you now confirm the buttons with the trigger, just
  like in every other menu.

• Lights no longer shine through walls. In one of the later levels, bright
  dots floated in front of walls and closed doors even though the lamp was
  behind them. The glow now only appears where the lamp is actually in view.

• Puzzles later in the game work again. In one level a door stayed shut even
  though the floor plates were set correctly — you could keep rearranging them
  and nothing happened. This affected several puzzles of that kind, and it grew
  more likely the further into the game you had come.

• The pause menu now always appears in front of you. Previously it stayed where
  it first showed up when you started the game, so if you had turned around, it
  was behind you.

• Lifts and switches work again after you die. Until now they stopped
  responding once you had died and reloaded, and even restarting from a save
  did not bring them back.

• After reloading, your weapon no longer keeps firing on its own, and the
  trigger stays usable. Before, you could end up unable to reload at all the
  next time you died, with no way out but quitting the app.

• Sense controllers are tracked again on older system versions. They did report
  button presses there, but did not move with you in the game.

• The app no longer quits unexpectedly while checking a large texture package.

WHAT WE KNOW

Loading a level takes a while, and the view stays black meanwhile. With the 4x
package it takes considerably longer.
