First build for Apple Vision Pro.

WHAT TO TRY

• Both titles: pick one in the launcher window, then "Play". Each has its own
  data folder, so both can be set up side by side.
• Game files: use "Choose files…" or copy them into the folder that "Open in
  Files" opens for you. The window names the files it is still missing.
• Motion control: swing, block and aim with the Sense controllers. Watch whether
  the blade sits where your hand actually is.
• Look around: lean, duck, step. Report anything that swims, doubles or lags
  behind your head.
• Menus and cut-scenes should stand still in the room while you move your head.

WHAT WE KNOW

Long level loads can take a while — the window stays black meanwhile.
